Private store administration

Verdura owner automation

An administration tool for an authorized operator to maintain Verdura’s configured Google Sheets catalog and support its order workflow.

Operated by Roman Sintsov. This site explains the tool’s purpose and its use of Google data. It does not collect Google passwords, start an OAuth login, or provide a customer checkout.

What the tool does

  • Checks the configured Products, Settings and Orders spreadsheet structure and reports bounded diagnostics.
  • Supports reviewed product-image updates and separately approved setup or maintenance operations.
  • Checks WhatsApp notification metadata and supports the store’s separately configured order-notification workflow.

Operations run through Google Apps Script. The local administration client verifies the configured project, deployment and operator identity before supported calls. It is not a general-purpose public spreadsheet editor.

Why Google authorization is requested

The operator authorizes access needed for configured spreadsheets, Apps Script maintenance, external service requests and outgoing fallback email. Google account identity is used to verify the authorized operator.

The spreadsheet permission is broader than the configured workbooks. Restrictions to the store’s configured files are enforced by the application, not by a Google selected-file permission. The tool does not read Gmail inbox contents.

Read the permission-by-permission explanation in the privacy policy before granting access.

Store customers do not sign in to Google

The customer application uses the store’s public catalog and order API. The owner administration login is separate; customers are not asked to grant these OAuth permissions.

The order runtime can save customer and order information in Google Sheets and send order summaries to the configured store recipient through WhatsApp/Meta and fallback email. The owner tool’s operator is identified here separately from the retail store operator.

For shopping and customer-order privacy, see the existing retail customer privacy policy and store support page. These information pages do not replace either page.
